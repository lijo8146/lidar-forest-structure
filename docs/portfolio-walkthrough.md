# Five-minute project walkthrough

1. Show products, then define the 250 m study window and mixed woodland/open-space scope.
2. Explain why horizontal feet and vertical US survey feet need separate handling. Open coordinate_plan and the manifest.
3. Show chunked ingestion and filtered counts; point out the memory limit and unchanged source.
4. Compare supplied ground and SMRF. Explain why disagreement is not accuracy; show the cross-section and local terrain range.
5. Show unsupported and negative heights. Explain why blank vegetation cells and return fractions cannot be translated directly into canopy cover or fuel.
6. Run tests/validation and point to hashes, parameters, compound CRS and the reproducibility commands.
7. Finish with the next scientific step: independent checkpoints, reviewed labels and field plots.
